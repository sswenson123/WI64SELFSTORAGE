Drive-Up Access Storage Units in New Richmond, WI

Pull your truck or trailer right to the door. Every unit, every size.

No Hallways. No Carts. No Elevators.

Every unit at WI 64 Self Storage is drive-up. You pull your pickup or trailer directly to your unit door, roll it up, and load. If you have ever hauled a couch down an indoor storage corridor, you already know why drive-up access is worth it.

Loading in minutes, not hours. Back the truck to the door — a full load that takes an afternoon at an indoor facility takes an hour here. Room for a truck AND trailer. Our aisles handle full-size pickups with trailers. Daily access, 6 AM to 10 PM. Grab the snowblower at 7 AM or drop off after work, every day. Every size is drive-up — from the $55 8x8 to the 10x26 that swallows a full household or a boat.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is drive-up storage?

A ground-level unit with a roll-up door you can park directly in front of, so you load straight from your vehicle into the unit.

Is drive-up storage cheaper than indoor storage?

Usually, yes — you are not paying for heated hallways. Our drive-up units start at $55 per month.

Can I fit my truck and trailer down the aisle?

Yes — our aisles are built for full-size pickups with trailers.

What are your access hours?

6 AM to 10 PM, every day of the week, including weekends and holidays.
