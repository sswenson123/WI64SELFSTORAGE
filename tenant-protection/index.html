Tenant Protection Plan

Protect what matters. The Bader Stored Property Program offers affordable coverage for your belongings — starting at just $12/month.

Why Get Coverage?

Your Belongings Deserve Protection

Standard storage leases limit facility liability. The Bader Program gives you direct coverage for your stored property against unexpected loss or damage.

🛡️

100% Coverage

Every plan covers 100% of your selected coverage amount — no partial payouts.

💵

Affordable Premiums

Plans start at just $12/month. Peace of mind for less than a cup of coffee a day.

📅

Month-to-Month

Coverage runs alongside your lease — no long-term commitment required.

📋

Simple Enrollment

Sign up at move-in with a quick participation form. Coverage starts immediately.

What's Covered ✓
  • ✓ Fire, lightning, explosion, smoke
  • ✓ Windstorm, hail, tornado, hurricane
  • ✓ Water damage (burst pipes / appliances)
  • ✓ Rodent damage
  • ✓ Vandalism & malicious mischief
  • ✓ Burglary (forced entry with visible marks)
  • ✓ Earthquake, sinkhole collapse
  • ✓ Flood (up to 50% of coverage limit / $2,000 max)
  • ✓ Transit within 100 miles (fire, collision)
  • ✓ Debris removal (25% of claim amount)
What's NOT Covered ✗
  • ✗ Jewelry, watches, precious stones, furs
  • ✗ Money, currency, securities, documents
  • ✗ Firearms, gold/silver/platinum
  • ✗ Theft or unexplained disappearance
  • ✗ Wear and tear, gradual deterioration
  • ✗ Insects, vermin (not rodent damage)
  • ✗ Mechanical breakdown
  • ✗ Sewer backup flood

This is a partial list. See the full Certificate of Property Insurance for all exclusions and conditions.
