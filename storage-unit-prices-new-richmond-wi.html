Price Factors

What Determines Storage Prices in New Richmond

Size is the biggest factor — but bigger is often the better per-square-foot deal: our 10x20 gives you 43% more space than the 10x14 for only $10 more per month. Access type matters too: drive-up units like ours typically cost less than indoor climate-controlled space while being far easier to load. Location: facilities closer to Hudson and the Twin Cities metro charge more; New Richmond rates like ours are among the lowest in St. Croix County. Season: summer moving season tightens availability — locking in a month-to-month rate in the off-season is the quiet money move.
