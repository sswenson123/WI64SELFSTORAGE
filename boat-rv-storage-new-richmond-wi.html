Boat, ATV & Vehicle Storage in New Richmond, WI

Fully enclosed winter storage minutes from the St. Croix — from $90/month.

Enclosed Storage Beats a Tarp in the Yard

When the boat comes off Cedar Lake or the St. Croix in October, it needs better than a tarp. Our 10x26 drive-up units give your boat, ATVs, or vehicle a fully enclosed, locked home for the winter — $130 a month, minutes from downtown New Richmond.

Wisconsin winters are hard on gel coats, upholstery, and covers. Inside a unit there is no snow load on the cover, no UV baking the seats, no mice moving in from an open field, and no spring surprise when you pull the tarp. Your insurance company likes enclosed storage better too.

What Fits

Pick Your Size

10x26 ($130/mo): fishing boats and runabouts to roughly 22–24 feet on trailer, full-size pickups, most cars plus gear. 10x20 ($90/mo): compact boats, jet skis on trailers, motorcycles, 2–3 ATVs or snowmobiles with trailer. Every unit is drive-up with wide aisles built for backing a boat.

Winter math: October through April is about 7 months. A 10x20 for the sleds and jet skis runs about $630 for the whole winter; a 10x26 for the boat about $910 — less than most marina winter packages, and you can get to your equipment any day from 6 AM to 10 PM (ice-fishing gear does not hibernate). Frequently Asked Questions

Can I store a boat on its trailer?

Yes — that is exactly what the 10x26 is sized for. Back the trailer straight to the door.

Do you offer winterization services?

We are storage only — have your boat winterized first (local marinas and shops handle it), then bring it over.

Can I check on my boat during the winter?

Yes, any day from 6 AM to 10 PM.

When should I reserve winter storage?

October fills fast — reserve in September and the spot is yours. You are month-to-month, so keep it into spring launch or let it go.
