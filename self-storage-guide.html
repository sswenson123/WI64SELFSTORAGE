Storage Packing Tips to Maximize Your Unit

Getting the most out of your storage unit comes down to smart packing. These tips can help you fit more in your unit and keep your items safer:

  • Use uniform box sizes — Standard-sized boxes stack better and make better use of vertical space.
  • Label everything clearly — Use large, legible labels on all four sides of each box. You'll thank yourself later.
  • Disassemble furniture — Tables, bed frames, and shelving units take up far less space when broken down. Keep hardware in labeled bags taped to the furniture piece.
  • Store items you'll need soonest near the door — Put the things you'll access regularly toward the front, and long-term items toward the back.
  • Use wardrobe boxes for clothing — These keep clothes wrinkle-free and hanging without taking up floor space.
  • Wrap fragile items well — Bubble wrap, packing paper, or even towels and blankets work great for protecting breakables.
  • Leave a walking aisle — A center aisle down the length of your unit makes it much easier to reach items in the back without unpacking everything.
  • Use the insides of drawers and appliances — Pack linens, towels, or soft items inside dressers and washing machines to maximize space.
