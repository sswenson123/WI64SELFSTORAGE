What Fits in a 10x20 Storage Unit?

Think of a standard one-car garage. A 10x20 comfortably holds the contents of a 2–3 bedroom home: living room set, two or three bedroom sets, appliances, dining table, and 40–50 boxes with a walkway down the middle. Or skip the furniture math: it fits a car, a compact pickup, a fishing boat on its trailer, or a couple of ATVs with room for gear along the walls.

Common uses: between-homes storage during a sale or build, a vehicle over winter, business inventory and job-site materials, an estate's full contents while the family sorts things out — or the garage's entire overflow so the actual car fits again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a 10x20 big enough for a 3-bedroom house?

For most 3-bedroom homes, yes — pack to the ceiling and use vertical space. Oversized or appliance-heavy households should step up to the 10x26.

Can I store a car in a 10x20?

Yes — nearly any sedan, SUV, or compact truck fits with room to walk around it.

Do I need insurance for my stored items?

Your homeowner's policy may cover stored goods; optional tenant protection is available at rental.
