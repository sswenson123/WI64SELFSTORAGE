📦 Unit Sizes & Availability

We offer four unit sizes: 8×8 (64 sq ft), 10×14 (140 sq ft), 10×20 (200 sq ft), and 10×26 (260 sq ft). Our most popular size is the 10×20, which fits the contents of a 2–3 bedroom home comfortably. Visit our Units page for a full breakdown.
Here's a quick guide:

• 8×8 — Great for boxes, seasonal decorations, or a few small furniture pieces
• 10×14 — Fits a studio or 1-bedroom apartment, or a garage cleanout
• 10×20 — Fits a 2–3 bedroom home; our most popular size
• 10×26 — Fits an entire home, or use it for a vehicle or business inventory

When in doubt, go one size up — it's better to have extra room than to run out of space.

🔒 Security & Access

The facility is accessible daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Contact us to confirm current hours, as they may vary.
While it's not always required, we strongly recommend protecting your stored items. We offer a Tenant Protection Plan through our portal. You may also check if your homeowner's or renter's insurance covers items in storage. Visit our Tenant Protection page to learn more.
For safety and legal reasons, the following items are not allowed in storage:

• Flammable or hazardous materials (gasoline, chemicals, propane)
• Food or perishables
• Living animals or plants
• Illegal items or stolen goods
• Firearms or ammunition (without proper authorization)
• Anything with strong odors that could affect neighboring units

When in doubt, contact us before storing something you're unsure about.
